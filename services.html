Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, we recommend booking 6–12 months ahead. For events and subscriptions, 2–4 weeks is usually plenty.

Do you offer delivery and setup?

Yes — delivery, installation, and day-of styling are included in all wedding and event packages.

Can I request specific flowers or colors?

Absolutely. We'll always work within what's seasonally available, but your palette and preferences guide every design.

What areas do you serve?

We deliver within a 50-mile radius of Springfield, with select travel available for larger events.
