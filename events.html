Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What types of events do you provide flowers for?

Private dinners, engagement parties, bridal showers, proposals, and corporate and brand events. If it’s a gathering, we can design for it.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend reaching out at least 2–4 weeks in advance to ensure availability and allow time for design planning, sourcing, and coordination. For larger events or brand initiatives, the more lead time, the better. If you don't need anything specific, we can accommodate events with as little as 24–48 hours' notice.

Is there a minimum spend for private events or brand activations?

Here are our starting rates for event florals:

  • Small Bud Vase (1–2 blooms)$30 each
  • Large Bud Vase (3–4 blooms)$50 each
  • Small/Cocktail Arrangement (4"x4" vase, ~8"x8" with florals)$95 each
  • Medium Arrangement (5"x5" vase, ~10"x10" with florals)$200 each
  • Large Arrangement (6"x6" vase, ~12"x12" with florals)$300 each
  • 2′–3′ Tall Bar Arrangement$300 each

Pricing depends on scale, complexity, and the number of arrangements needed. We’ll work with you to create a custom quote that aligns with your needs and budget. To receive a detailed quote and learn more about our minimums, please fill out our contact form with a few details about your event. Our team will follow up with personalized recommendations and pricing guidance.

Can I request specific flowers or colors?

Absolutely. We'll always work within what's available, but your palette and preferences guide every design decision.

Do you handle delivery and setup?

Yes — delivery, installation, and day-of styling are included in every event proposal.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in New York and Rhode Island, with travel available for events further out — just ask.

Tell us about your event and we'll create a custom floral proposal.
