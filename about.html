Founder, arranging flowers
A New Independent Studio

Design led by artistic vision, not a fixed catalog.

Intentions started with a simple fascination — color, texture, and the way a single arrangement can transform a room. That curiosity turned into a growing body of work, then into a small independent studio.

We're new, and we're proud of it. Being small means every arrangement is still composed by hand, one piece at a time, with room to experiment rather than repeat the same designs over and over.

"Our work is guided by color, form, and texture — florals composed with the eye of a designer, not assembled from a catalog."
