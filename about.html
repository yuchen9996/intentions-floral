Studio Founder
Since 2018

A studio built on seasonal flowers and honest design.

Bloom & Co. started as a single kitchen-table operation making bouquets for friends' weddings. Today, we're a full-service floral design studio — but the philosophy hasn't changed: work with what's in season, source close to home, and let the flowers do the talking.

Every arrangement that leaves our studio is designed by hand, one stem at a time, by a small team who genuinely loves this work.
