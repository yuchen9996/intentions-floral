Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?

As a small studio, we take on a limited number of events at a time — we recommend reaching out as soon as you have a date, even if the rest of your details aren't finalized yet.

Can I request specific flowers or colors?

Absolutely. Availability can shift, but your palette and preferences guide every design decision.

Do you handle delivery and setup?

Yes — delivery, installation, and day-of styling are included in every wedding and event proposal.

What areas do you serve?

We're based between New York City and Rhode Island, with travel available for events further out — just ask.

Planning something beautiful?

Tell us about your date, venue, and vision and we'll create a custom floral proposal.
