Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What’s the difference between intimate, à la carte, and full service?

Intimate and micro weddings cover the essentials for a small celebration, such as a bridal bouquet, boutonniere, and a few pieces. À la carte lets you order only the florals you need. Full service is a complete floral design for the whole wedding, including delivery, setup, and breakdown.

Can I order just a bouquet?

Yes. Our à la carte option is designed for exactly that — share what you need and we’ll quote it.

How far in advance should I book?

As a small studio, we take on a limited number of weddings at a time, so we recommend reaching out as soon as you have a date, even if the rest of your details aren’t finalized yet. We thoughtfully design each wedding to suit the venue and the vibe, and the earlier you share your vision with us, the better we can create a truly bespoke design.

Can I request specific flowers or colors?

Absolutely. We'll always work within what's available, but your palette and preferences guide every design decision.

Do you handle delivery and setup?

Full-service weddings include delivery, setup, and breakdown. For intimate and à la carte orders, delivery and setup can be added — just ask.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in New York and Rhode Island, with travel available for weddings further out — just ask.
